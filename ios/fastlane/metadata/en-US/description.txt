Bookdrive is an audiobook player for the files you already keep in Google Drive.

Connect your Google Drive, choose the folder where your audiobooks live, then stream or download them for offline listening. Bookdrive remembers your position, keeps background audio going, supports lock-screen controls, and helps organize large libraries without moving files into a new service.

Features:

- Stream audiobooks from Google Drive
- Download files for offline listening
- Resume from your last position
- Background playback and lock-screen controls
- Chapters, sleep timer, and playback speed
- Cover lookup from public book databases
- Progress export and import

Bookdrive is for personal media libraries. It does not include, sell, or distribute audiobook content.
